Refund Policy

Last updated：2026-10-26

iOS app (App Store purchases)

In-app purchases are paid to Apple, so refunds follow Apple's rules: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple ID you used. After a refund the Full Version locks again and your progress is kept. If anything goes wrong, email hello@emptyoffice.app and we'll help.

Web version

The web version is currently free, so there is nothing to refund. If it ever becomes paid, web orders follow these rules:

  • Within 14 days of purchase, email us for any reason and we will give you a full refund.
  • After 14 days, if a technical problem stops you using it and we cannot fix it, we will still refund you.
  • Email hello@emptyoffice.app with the email address you used and your order number; Lemon Squeezy returns the money to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
  • After a refund the key is disabled and paid content locks again; your progress is kept.
